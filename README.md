**PCI CARD**




```
Standard PCIe card PCB thickness is 1.6 mm.
PCI and PCIe slots are designed for that thickness — ensures mechanical compatibility.
Some low-profile or custom cards may use 1.2 mm or 0.8 mm, but 1.6 mm is industry standard..


For PCIe edge connector (gold fingers), the recommended finished thickness is:

✅ 1.6 mm ± 0.15 mm

This ensures perfect fit in standard PCIe slots.
Gold plating: usually 30 µin to 50 µin ENIG or hard gold.
```
